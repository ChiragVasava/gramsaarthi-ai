### [0:35 - 1:15] Module 2: Deterministic Financial Engine & Scheme Routing
- **Visual**: Financial Calculator (`/calculator`).
- **Narrator**:
  > *"Instead of hallucinating financial numbers with an LLM, GramSaarthi employs a deterministic statutory financial engine. Rajesh enters ₹1,00,000 as his 10% equity margin. Notice the 6-month moratorium grace period before quarterly EMI amortization starts."*
- **Action**: Change margin to ₹12,000 to demonstrate instant auto-routing to the **Micro Finance Scheme (6.5%)**, then return to ₹1,00,000.

### [1:15 - 1:55] Module 1: Hyper-Local Market & Competitor Mapping
- **Visual**: Market & Competitor Map (`/map`).
- **Narrator**:
  > *"Module 1 solves local feasibility. Here we examine Savli block in Vadodara. The 5 km core catchment encompasses 4,200 residents, while the 10 km secondary ring touches institutional buyers and the Amul milk collection hub just 2.4 km away. GramSaarthi maps surrounding competitors and identifies that while raw milk competition is moderate, value-addition into Ghee and Paneer is 100% unserved in this village cluster."*
- **Action**: Toggle map filters (All, Dairy, Offtake) to showcase spatial intelligence.

---

### [1:55 - 2:35] AI Business Feasibility Dossier & Multilingual Advisor
- **Visual**: Full Feasibility Report (`/report`) and AI Chat (`/chat`).
- **Narrator**:
  > *"GramSaarthi compiles this into an institutional-grade Feasibility Dossier—complete with an 84/100 viability score, SWOT matrix, and seasonal risk warnings. Notice that the entrepreneur can download an official PDF with a single click—no printing setup required. The candidate can also consult the **Multilingual AI Advisor** in English, Hindi, or Gujarati. The advisor greets them by their personal name and is strictly guarded to answer only rural enterprise questions—ensuring safe, reliable institutional advisory."*
- **Action**: 
  1. Click **"Download Official PDF"** on `/report` to show direct client-side PDF generation.
  2. Switch language to **Hindi (HI)** or **Gujarati (GU)** using the top language switcher to demonstrate live multilingual localization.
  3. Open AI Chat (`/chat`), ask *"What is the best pricing for value-added Ghee vs raw milk?"* to show actionable rural business advice.
  4. Ask an off-topic question like *"Write code in Python to reverse a string"* to show the polite AI domain guardrail in action!
